# Archived: sound hero

Black, white and grain, with a hand-drawn-looking waveform seeded from the song playing now (data from
`now-playing.json`), a lowercase mono "now playing" caption, a scrubbable playhead and a flickering film-grain
layer. Replaced by the search hero. To bring it back: copy `hero.js` and `hero.css` to the project root, put
`index-hero-markup.html` in place of the hero `<section>` in `index.html`, link `hero.css`/`hero.js` instead of
`search.css`/`search.js` (and list them in `.github/workflows/pages.yml`).
