About

I’m a product designer with 10+ years of experience shipping digital products across big tech, fintech, agencies and early-stage startups. Today I’m a UX designer at Google, based in São Paulo.

I’m comfortable owning a project end to end: discovery and research, interaction and visual design, prototyping, and handoff. I’m known for going beyond my scope to align stakeholders, unblock teams and advocate for users.

I love creating solutions from scratch and imagining where a product could go next, always in close collaboration with my team. I pick up context fast, build clear narratives, and naturally become the person people come to for a jam session. I bring optimism and care to every room, and I do my best work when I understand the why behind it.
